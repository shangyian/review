Back-of-the-Envelope

Random estimation problems with round numbers. Type a quick estimate, see how many times off you were, and read the worked steps. Within a factor of two counts as good, because that is what a decision needs.

    Numbers like 1500, 1.5k, 2M, 3B and 1e6 are accepted. Scores use the ratio between your answer and the exact one, so 800 against 1,000 is 1.25 times off, the same as 1,250. Your progress is kept in this browser only.

    Numbers worth memorizing

    Time

    • 1 day = 86,400 s, close to 105
    • 1 month is about 2.5 million s
    • 1 year is about 31.5 million s, close to 3 × 107

    Size

    • KB 103, MB 106, GB 109, TB 1012, PB 1015 bytes
    • 8 bits per byte, so 1 Gbps = 125 MB/s

    Rules

    • Concurrency = rate × time (Little's law)
    • Average read = hits × fast + misses × slow
    • A 95% range is about 2 standard errors

    Latency, rounded and typical

    • Same-datacenter hop: about 0.5 ms
    • Across an ocean: about 150 ms
    • See the Latency Ladder

    Defaults used in problems

    • Postgres allows about 100 connections by default
    • One worker finishes 1 / (time per request) per second
