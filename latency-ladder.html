Latency Ladder

Two ladders, because two different things get measured: how long one small operation takes, and how long it takes to move 1 MB. Bars share one log scale, so each gridline is 10× the last. The grey figure is the same delay if 1 nanosecond lasted 1 second.

    For one small operation, RAM is 200× slower than L1. An SSD read is 1,000× slower than RAM. A datacenter round trip is 5× slower than that, a disk seek 20× slower again, and crossing the ocean 15× slower than the seek.

    Notes: SSD random read is quoted as about 100 µs; real drives range from roughly 16 to 150 µs. The 1 MB network figure is computed from bandwidth (8 megabits at 1 Gbps), not measured. Moving 1 MB is mostly transfer time, so it says little about the wait for a single small request. The ocean round trip is 150 ms, which is 150,000,000 ns.
