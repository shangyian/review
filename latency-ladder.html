Latency Ladder

Every rung is slower than the one above it. Bars sit on one log scale, so each gridline is 10× the last. The grey figure on each row is the same delay if 1 nanosecond lasted 1 second.

    RAM is 200× slower than L1. A datacenter round trip is 5,000× slower than RAM. A disk seek is 20× slower than that, and crossing the ocean is 15× slower again.

    Source list corrected: it gave the California–Netherlands–California round trip as 150,000 ns, but 150 ms is 150,000,000 ns, which is what the bar uses. The SSD figure is read as 1 MB sequential, matching the disk rows.
